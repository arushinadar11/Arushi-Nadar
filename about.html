about me.

Hi, I’m Arushi Nadar, a visual designer.

Stories, shaped into things people can hold and use.

I am a visual designer working across product and game design, brand identity, packaging and digital interfaces. I approach each project through research and storytelling, translating ideas into clear, considered outcomes through form, colour and material.

Before beginning my Master of Design at Griffith University in Brisbane, I interned at Landor India, where I contributed to Coca-Cola’s festive season campaign. The experience showed me how a single brand idea can be adapted with care for different regions, languages and formats.

I am drawn to design that is considered, tactile and meaningful: objects and experiences that invite people to engage, connect and return to them. I enjoy taking an idea from early sketches through to a resolved, tested outcome, whether that is a physical prototype, a brand system or an interface.
